### FAQ（8 组，可直配 FAQPage schema）
1. **Q: What does FOB Shenzhen include when I buy used cars from China?**
   A: FOB Shenzhen covers the vehicle, pre-shipment inspection, export customs clearance in China and loading onto the vessel at Shekou/Yantian. Ocean freight, insurance and destination charges are yours — or switch to CIF Mombasa/Umm Qasr and we quote them included.
2. **Q: How do you verify second hand cars from China before payment?**
   A: Every unit ships with a photo-complete inspection report: 168-point checklist, verified odometer, battery health test for EVs, and interior/exterior photo set. We also accept third-party inspectors at our yard before container loading.
3. **Q: What documents do I receive with each shipment?**
   A: Commercial invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Inspection (COI where required), export declaration, and — for EVs — battery/un3480 handling papers your clearing agent needs.
4. **Q: Do you sell single cars or only wholesale?**
   A: We serve B2B buyers: minimum order is one container slot, typically 4–6 SUVs or 8–10 compact cars. Mixed-brand, mixed-fuel loads are welcome and common.
5. **Q: Can I ship used EV export China units to Kenya?**
   A: Yes. We ship BYD EVs to Mombasa on CIF terms monthly, with state-of-charge compliance, COI and battery documentation prepared for KEBS and port clearance.
6. **Q: How is the price of used BYD cars for export calculated?**
   A: Chinese market wholesale price for year/mileage/condition, converted at the day's rate. No hidden retail markup — our FOB price list updates as fleet units arrive weekly.
7. **Q: Which destination ports do you cover?**
   A: Regular CIF service to Mombasa (Kenya), Umm Qasr (Iraq) and Jebel Ali (UAE transshipment); FOB Shenzhen worldwide. Roro options for oversize units on request.
8. **Q: How long does shipping take?**
   A: Shekou → Mombasa typically 25–32 days; → Jebel Ali 18–24 days; → Umm Qasr 28–35 days, subject to sailing schedules. We confirm the exact vessel ETA with every booking.
