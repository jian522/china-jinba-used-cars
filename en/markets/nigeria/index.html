Why used BYD cars for export top Nigerian demand lists

Fuel prices pushed Nigerian buyers toward efficient EVs and hybrids, while petrol SUVs still anchor family retail. Our stock covers both: BYD city EVs from $6,639 FOB and 7-seat petrol SUVs under $12,000 — pre-inspected so reconditioning surprises don't eat your margin.

Landed cost: FOB + freight + duty (20%) + levies + port fees

Plan your Apapa or Tin Can landed cost from four numbers: FOB Shenzhen vehicle price, ocean freight to Lagos (we quote transshipment via Mombasa or direct where available), the 20% import duty plus levies on CIF value, and port/clearing charges. We supply the document set — B/L, invoice, packing list, export declaration — that your agent files.

Bulk shipment beats single-unit buying

One 40ft container takes 4–6 SUVs or 8–10 compact cars. Sharing freight across 6+ units typically cuts per-unit shipping cost by 30–45% versus single-car consolidations — the margin difference that keeps Nigerian lots profitable.

FAQ

What is the vehicle age rule for Nigerian imports?

Nigeria applies tariff penalties to vehicles over a set age bracket; 2021–2024 stock sits in the lowest band. Confirm current rates with your clearing agent before ordering — rules change.

How do I verify cars before paying?

Every listing carries a photo-complete 168-point inspection report. Third-party inspection in Shenzhen is welcome before container loading.

Do you ship directly to Lagos?

We ship FOB Shenzhen worldwide and arrange bulk shipments to Lagos via transshipment (typically Mombasa or Colombo). Your agent handles discharge and clearing.

Which documents ship with the container?

Bill of Lading, commercial invoice, packing list and export declaration — issued to match your clearing agent's filing requirements.
