Why Kenyan dealers buy wholesale used cars from China

BYD EVs and Chinese petrol SUVs deliver a price-to-condition ratio local supply cannot match. Our FOB Shenzhen price list updates weekly as fleet units arrive, and mixed EV + petrol containers let you serve both city buyers and upcountry retail.

CIF Mombasa: what the price includes

A CIF Mombasa quotation covers the vehicle, pre-shipment inspection, export clearance in China, ocean freight and insurance to Mombasa. Kenyan import duty, IDF and RDL, and port handling at Mombasa remain on your account — we hand your clearing agent a complete document set: B/L, COI, invoice and packing list.

KEBS & pre-shipment inspection

Vehicles bound for Kenya are inspected to KEBS requirements with a Certificate of Inspection issued before sailing. EVs ship with state-of-charge compliance and battery documentation so port clearance stays on schedule.

FAQ

How long does shipping from Shenzhen to Mombasa take?

Typically 25–32 days port to port, subject to the sailing schedule. We confirm the exact vessel ETA when you book.

Can I buy a mixed container of EVs and petrol SUVs?

Yes — mixed loads are our standard format. Most Kenyan clients combine 2–4 BYD EVs with 2–4 petrol SUVs in one 40ft container.

Do you quote CIF Mombasa or only FOB?

We quote both. FOB Shenzhen if you have your own freight forwarder; CIF Mombasa with freight and insurance included if you want one number to plan against.

What documents does my clearing agent receive?

Bill of Lading, Certificate of Inspection (COI), commercial invoice, packing list and export declaration — issued to match your agent's requirements.
