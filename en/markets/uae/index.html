FAQ

Do UAE buyers need any special EV documents?

Standard export set plus battery/UN 3480 papers for sea booking. Your forwarder handles UAE import compliance; we supply everything the carrier requires.

How long is the Shenzhen–Jebel Ali transit?

Typically 18–24 days port to port, with weekly sailings. We confirm the vessel and ETA at booking.

Can I inspect the cars before loading?

Yes — third-party inspection in Shenzhen is welcome, and every unit already carries a 168-point photo report.

What mileage is typical in your EV stock?

Most BYD units sit between 9,000 and 40,000 km — verified against service records and the odometer photo set.
