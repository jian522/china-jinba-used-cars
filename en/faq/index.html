Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about buying used cars from China.

General Questions

How do I verify a Chinese used car before purchasing?

We recommend requesting a comprehensive inspection report including vehicle history, service records, and third-party inspection. Jinba Auto Export provides detailed vehicle reports and can arrange pre-purchase inspections.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept T/T bank transfer, LC (Letter of Credit), and secure escrow services. All payments are processed through verified banking channels with full documentation.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping times vary by destination: Southeast Asia (7-20 days), Middle East (18-35 days), Africa (25-45 days), South America (30-50 days). We provide tracking and updates throughout the process.

Vehicle Questions

What brands do you export?

We specialize in Chinese brands including BYD, Chery, Geely, Haval, NIO, XPeng, Li Auto, MG, and more. Each brand page on our website shows current inventory.

Are the vehicles in good condition?

All vehicles undergo rigorous inspection before listing. We provide detailed reports including mileage verification, mechanical condition, and photo documentation.

Do you offer warranties?

Warranty terms vary by vehicle and brand. We coordinate with manufacturers and local dealers to provide after-sales support in your country.

Export & Shipping

Can you help with import documentation?

Absolutely. We prepare all necessary export documents including commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, bill of lading, and any other required paperwork for your country's customs.

Do you arrange shipping?

Yes, we coordinate with reputable freight forwarders to arrange sea freight (container or RO-RO) to your destination port. We can also arrange door-to-door delivery if needed.

What are the shipping costs?

Shipping costs depend on destination, vehicle size, and shipping method. We provide quotes based on your specific requirements.

After-Sales Support

Do you provide after-sales support?

Yes, we coordinate with local dealers and service centers in your country for warranty support and spare parts. We also provide technical documentation and training materials.

How do I get spare parts?

We can arrange spare parts shipment alongside vehicles or separately. Many Chinese brands have established parts distribution networks in major markets.

Can you customize vehicles to local standards?

In some cases, yes. We work with manufacturers and export specialists to ensure vehicles meet your country's requirements.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is ready to help you with any questions about buying used cars from China.
