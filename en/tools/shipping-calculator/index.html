Shipping Options Explained

Container Shipping

Ideal for 1-2 vehicles. Your car is secured in a 20ft or 40ft container, protected from weather and damage during transit.

  • Maximum protection
  • Door-to-door possible
  • Cost: $1,500-$3,000

RoRo Shipping

Roll-on/Roll-off for bulk shipments. Vehicles are driven onto the ship and secured on deck. Most economical for multiple vehicles.

  • Cost-effective for 3+ vehicles
  • Quick loading/unloading
  • Cost: $800-$1,500 per vehicle

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Shipping times vary by destination: Middle East (18-30 days), Africa (25-45 days), South America (30-50 days), Southeast Asia (7-20 days).

What documents are needed?

We handle all export documentation including commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and certificate of origin.

Is insurance included?

Basic marine insurance is available. We recommend full coverage insurance for valuable vehicles.
