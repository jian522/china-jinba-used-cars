Frequently Asked Questions

How is the customs duty calculated?

Customs duty is typically calculated as a percentage of the vehicle's CIF value (Cost, Insurance, and Freight). Some countries also consider the vehicle's age and engine size.

Are there any exemptions?

Some countries offer duty exemptions for electric vehicles, diplomatic vehicles, or under specific trade agreements. Check with your local customs authority.

What other costs should I budget for?

Budget for shipping costs ($800-$3,000), insurance (1-2% of vehicle value), port charges, customs broker fees, and registration costs.
