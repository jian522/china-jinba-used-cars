Use this checklist to discuss sourcing a used vehicle from China. Each vehicle, route and destination must be checked separately.

Before paying, confirm the vehicle identity, ownership, current availability, year, mileage, condition and available photos. Request an independent inspection when appropriate.

Ask for a written quote that separates vehicle price, trade term, export port, freight, insurance and destination charges. Shipping time and required documents depend on the carrier and destination.

Check import eligibility, steering position, age, emissions, taxes and local registration with the destination authority or a licensed customs broker.
