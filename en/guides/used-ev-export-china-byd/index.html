Used EV export from China has moved from niche to mainstream, and one brand keeps topping dealer buy-lists: BYD. For importers across Kenya, Nigeria, the UAE and Iraq, used BYD cars for export combine a real price advantage with battery technology that holds up in hot climates. This guide explains what to check before you buy — and how we de-risk every used EV export China shipment.

The price ladder: Seagull to Han. Our current EV stock runs $6,639–$13,889 FOB Shenzhen: Seagull (city runabout, fleet-favourite), Dolphin (compact, best turn-rate), Yuan Plus/Atto 3 (compact SUV, taxi-proven), and Han (flagship sedan for premium retail). This ladder lets one container serve three retail tiers.

Blade Battery in used cars: what inspection covers. Battery health testing is part of our 168-point checklist: state-of-health reading, charge/discharge behaviour, thermal management check, and a physical look at the pack casing. EVs ship with state of charge set to shipping-compliant levels and UN 3480 handling papers for the carrier.

Hot-climate performance. Fleet feedback from Gulf and East African operators: range loss in 40°C+ operation is modest, cabin cooling loads are manageable, and the LFP chemistry tolerates heat cycles better than older NMC packs — a practical reason BYD retains resale value at destination.

Charging standards & adapters. BYD export units use GB/T DC charging; destination markets use CCS2 (Kenya, UAE) or Type 2 AC. Budget for adapter sets or an AC wallbox per unit — we advise the exact kit per destination when you book.

Parts & service support. BYD's authorized network now covers Kenya, Nigeria and the UAE, and common wear parts (filters, suspension, brake components) are increasingly stocked locally. For EV-specific items, we supply the parts directory and VIN so your service partner can pre-order.

Shipping EVs: UN 3480 rules. EVs move as Class 9 dangerous goods in container: state of charge capped (typically ≤30%), battery documentation, carrier pre-approval, and segregation rules inside the container plan. We handle the paperwork chain so your forwarder only books.

Build a mixed EV + petrol container. The profitable pattern our African clients repeat: 2–4 BYD EVs (city tier) + 2–4 petrol SUVs (family tier) + 1–2 hybrids in one 40ft. EVs turn fast in cities, petrol SUVs anchor upcountry retail — one shipment, two demand curves.
