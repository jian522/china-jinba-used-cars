What $10,000 actually buys

Under $10,000 the export market is dominated by compact sedans, small SUVs and the first generation of Chinese electric cars. Most units in that band are 2019-2022 model years with 30,000-90,000 km. Condition varies far more at this price than at $20,000, so ask for a dated photo set and an inspection report on the exact unit, not on the model in general.

Models that commonly trade in this band

  • Geely Emgrand - sedan on a Volvo-derived platform, simple mechanicals, widely available parts.
  • Changan CS55 - compact SUV, usually the cheapest credible high-clearance option.
  • Haval H6 (older years) - larger SUV body, but early-year examples sit at the top of this budget.
  • BYD e5 / Qin EV - electric sedans, cheap to run, but battery health must be documented.

What the price includes - and what it does not

A quoted number is only comparable when the trade term is stated. FOB covers the vehicle and loading at the Chinese port. CIF adds ocean freight and marine insurance to your destination port. Neither covers import duty, clearing agents, port storage or inland transport at your end. Two suppliers quoting '$8,500' can differ by several thousand dollars once the term and destination are clear.

Questions to ask before paying a deposit

  • Is the quote FOB or CIF, and to which port?
  • Does the unit have a documented battery state of health (for EVs)?
  • Which model year and which drive configuration - left-hand drive is required in most African markets?
  • What is the exact VIN, and can you receive photos of that VIN?

Answer these in writing before any payment. A supplier who cannot answer them is not ready to export.
