How to Import Used Cars to Africa from China

Africa is one of the fastest-growing markets for Chinese used cars. Discover how to import quality vehicles to Kenya, Nigeria, Tanzania, South Africa, and other African countries.

January 1, 2026·15 min read

Why African Markets Love Chinese Cars

Chinese used cars have become increasingly popular across Africa due to their affordability, durability, and improving quality. Key factors driving this trend:

  • Affordable Pricing: Chinese vehicles are 30-50% cheaper than Japanese equivalents
  • Durability: Modern Chinese cars are built for various road conditions
  • Availability of Parts: Growing network of spare parts suppliers across Africa
  • Government Support: Many African countries encourage Chinese investment and trade

Top African Markets for Chinese Cars

Kenya 🇰🇪

Kenya is one of East Africa's largest auto markets. Mombasa port handles most vehicle imports. Chinese brands like Chery, Geely, and Haval are gaining market share. Duty ranges from 25% for hybrid/EV to 100% for ICE vehicles depending on age and engine size.

Nigeria 🇳🇬

Nigeria is Africa's largest auto market. Lagos and Onitsha are key entry points. Chinese brands have established strong footholds with models like Chery Tiggo, Geely Emgrand, and Haval H6. Import duty is approximately 35% for used vehicles.

Tanzania 🇹🇿

Dar es Salaam port serves as a gateway to landlocked countries. Chinese cars are popular for their value and reliability. Duty rates vary by vehicle type and age.

South Africa 🇿🇦

South Africa has strict SABS standards but growing demand for affordable Chinese vehicles. Main ports: Durban, Cape Town, Port Elizabeth.

Ghana 🇬🇭

Tema and Takoradi ports handle vehicle imports. Chinese cars are popular among budget-conscious buyers. Duty ranges from 20-35% depending on vehicle specifications.

Shipping from China to Africa

Main shipping routes:

  • China to Mombasa (Kenya): 25-35 days via container or RO-RO
  • China to Lagos (Nigeria): 30-45 days via West Africa routes
  • China to Durban (South Africa): 30-40 days
  • China to Dar es Salaam (Tanzania): 25-35 days

Shipping costs: $1,200-$2,500 per vehicle depending on destination and shipping method.

Best Chinese Brands for African Markets

Chery

Excellent presence in Africa with models like Tiggo 4, Tiggo 7, and Arrizo 5. Strong after-sales network.

Geely

Affordable and reliable vehicles popular in East and West Africa. Coolray and Preface models gaining traction.

Haval

Great Wall's SUV specialist with strong performance in South and East Africa. H6 and H9 are bestsellers.

BYD

Leading EV manufacturer with growing presence. Attractive for countries with EV incentives.

Tips for African Importers

  1. Research local regulations: Each country has different requirements
  2. Choose right-spec vehicles: Ensure compliance with local standards
  3. Build relationships: Work with reliable suppliers and freight forwarders
  4. Plan for parts: Ensure spare parts availability in your market
  5. Consider financing: Explore import financing options
