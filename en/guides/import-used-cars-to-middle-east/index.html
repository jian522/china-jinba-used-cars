How to Import Used Cars to Middle East from China

A complete guide for importing quality used vehicles from China to Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, UAE, and other Middle Eastern countries.

January 1, 2026·12 min read

Why Import Used Cars from China to Middle East?

The Middle East has become one of the most important markets for Chinese used car exports. Key advantages include:

  • Cultural Proximity: Strong China-Middle East trade relations
  • Competitive Pricing: Chinese vehicles 30-50% cheaper than Japanese equivalents
  • Hot Climate Adaptation: Modern Chinese cars built for high temperatures
  • Growing Demand: Rising middle class seeking affordable vehicles

Country-by-Country Guide

Saudi Arabia 🇸🇦

Saudi Arabia is the largest auto market in the Middle East. Key ports: Jeddah Islamic Port, King Abdulaziz Port (Dammam). Import duty: 5% for most vehicles. Age limit: 5 years for used cars.

Qatar 🇶🇦

Doha Port handles most vehicle imports. Chinese brands gaining popularity. Import duty: 5%. Quality standards similar to UAE.

Kuwait 🇰🇼

Shuwaikh Port is the main entry point. Strict inspection requirements. Import duty: 5-12% depending on vehicle value.

Oman 🇴🇲

Port of Salalah handles vehicle imports. Growing market for Chinese brands. Import duty: 5%.

Bahrain 🇧🇭

Fairwell Port handles imports. Small but lucrative market. Import duty: 5%.

Import Requirements

Common Requirements Across GCC Countries

  • Vehicle age limit: Usually 5 years or less
  • Left-hand drive required
  • Emission standards: Euro 4 or higher
  • Customs duty: Generally 5%
  • VAT: 5% in most Gulf countries

Required Documents

  1. Commercial invoice
  2. Packing list
  3. Bill of lading
  4. Certificate of origin
  5. Insurance certificate
  6. Import license (for commercial importers)

Tips for Middle East Importers

  1. Climate Considerations: Ensure vehicles have proper AC and cooling systems
  2. Sand Protection: Check air filtration systems
  3. Parts Availability: Choose brands with local service networks
  4. Payment Terms: Use secure payment methods
  5. Local Regulations: Verify requirements for each country
