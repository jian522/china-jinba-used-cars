How to Import Used Cars to South America from China

South America is emerging as a major market for Chinese used car exports. Learn about import procedures for Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, and other countries.

January 1, 2026·14 min read

Why South America is Booming for Chinese Car Exports

South America has become one of the fastest-growing markets for Chinese vehicle exports. Key factors:

  • Trade Relations: Strong China-Latin America trade partnerships
  • Affordable Pricing: Chinese vehicles 40-60% cheaper than alternatives
  • Growing Middle Class: Increasing demand for affordable transportation
  • EV Adoption: Many countries offering EV incentives

Country-by-Country Guide

Brazil 🇧🇷

Brazil is South America's largest auto market. Main ports: Santos, Rio de Janeiro. Chinese brands like MG, Geely, and BYD have established presence. Import duty: up to 35% for used vehicles. Age restrictions apply.

Argentina 🇦🇷

Buenos Aires port handles most vehicle imports. Chinese brands gaining market share. Import regulations can be complex due to currency controls.

Chile 🇨🇱

Valparaiso and San Antonio ports. One of the most open markets in South America. Import duty: 6% for used cars. Age limit: 10 years.

Colombia 🇨🇴

Cartagena and Buenaventura ports. Import duty: 10% for used vehicles. Age limit: 10 years.

Peru 🇵🇪

Callao Port is the main entry point. Import duty: 16% for used cars. Age limit: 10 years.

Import Requirements

General Requirements

  • Vehicle age limits vary by country (5-10 years typical)
  • Left-hand drive required in most countries
  • Emission standards: Vary by country
  • Import duties: 6-35% depending on country and vehicle type

Required Documents

  1. Commercial invoice
  2. Packing list
  3. Bill of lading
  4. Certificate of origin
  5. Insurance certificate
  6. Import license (varies by country)

Tips for South American Importers

  1. Research local regulations: Each country has different requirements
  2. Consider humidity: Vehicles should have proper corrosion protection
  3. Build local partnerships: Work with established dealers
  4. Plan for parts: Ensure spare parts availability
  5. Understand currency risks: Consider payment in USD or CNY
