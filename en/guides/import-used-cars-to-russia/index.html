How to Import Used Cars to Russia from China

Russia has become one of the largest importers of Chinese used cars. Learn about the requirements, procedures, and opportunities in this growing market.

January 1, 2026·12 min read

The Rise of Chinese Cars in Russia

Since 2022, Chinese automakers have rapidly expanded their presence in the Russian market. With Western brands departing, Chinese used cars now dominate new vehicle sales in Russia. This trend extends to the used car import sector, where Chinese vehicles are increasingly popular due to their competitive pricing and improving quality.

Russian Import Regulations

Technical Regulation TR CU 018/2011

All vehicles imported to Russia must comply with the Technical Regulation of the Customs Union "On the Safety of Wheeled Vehicles". Key requirements include:

  • Euro 5 or Euro 6 emission standards (depending on vehicle age)
  • Valid STS (vehicle registration certificate)
  • ELPTS (electronic vehicle passport) for new vehicles
  • Safety compliance certification

Age Restrictions

Russia has specific age limits for imported used vehicles:

  • Vehicles up to 5 years old: Standard import procedures
  • Vehicles 5-10 years old: Additional certification may be required
  • Vehicles over 10 years old: Restricted import for personal use

Popular Chinese Brands in Russia

Chery

One of the most popular Chinese brands in Russia with extensive dealer network. Models like Tiggo series and Arrizo are well-received.

Haval

Great Wall Motors' premium brand with strong presence in Russia. H6 and H9 models are popular among Russian buyers.

Geely

Affordable and reliable vehicles with good after-sales support. The Coolray and Preface models are gaining popularity.

MG

British heritage, Chinese manufacturing. MG provides good value with accessible pricing and decent quality.

Import Procedures

  1. Vehicle Selection: Choose vehicles from verified Chinese suppliers
  2. Documentation: Prepare commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin
  3. Shipping: Arrange transport via rail (Trans-Siberian) or sea ports
  4. Customs Clearance: Submit documents to Russian customs
  5. Certification: Obtain PTS (vehicle passport) and safety certificate
  6. Registration: Register vehicle with GTSA (traffic police)

Shipping Routes

Main shipping options from China to Russia:

  • Rail Transport: China-Europe railway express, 14-20 days to Moscow
  • Sea + Rail: Port of Vladivostok to Moscow, 20-30 days total
  • Direct Rail: From Chinese ports to major Russian cities

Shipping costs vary by route and vehicle type, typically $1,500-$3,000 per vehicle.

Tips for Russian Market

  1. Winter Preparation: Ensure vehicles have proper cold-weather packages
  2. Right-Hand Drive: Not an issue for Chinese exports (LHD vehicles)
  3. Parts Availability: Choose brands with established service networks
  4. Currency Risk: Consider RUB/CNY exchange rate fluctuations
  5. Payment Terms: Use secure payment methods and escrow services
