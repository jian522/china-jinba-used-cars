How to Import Used Cars to UAE from China

A complete guide for dealers and buyers looking to import quality used vehicles from China to the United Arab Emirates.

January 1, 2026·10 min read

Why Import Used Cars from China to UAE?

China has become one of the world's largest automobile markets, producing high-quality vehicles at competitive prices. For UAE importers and dealers, Chinese used cars offer significant advantages:

  • Competitive Pricing: Chinese vehicles are typically 20-40% cheaper than equivalent Japanese or Korean models
  • Modern Technology: Chinese EVs and hybrids lead in battery technology and smart features
  • Wide Selection: BYD, NIO, XPeng, Li Auto, Geely, Chery, and more brands available
  • Quality Standards: Major Chinese manufacturers meet international safety and emission standards

UAE Import Requirements

Emirates Authority for Standardization and Metrology (ESMA)

All imported vehicles must comply with ESMA standards. Key requirements include:

  • Vehicle must meet UAE specification standards (GOSI certification)
  • Age limit: Vehicles must not be older than 5 years for most categories
  • Right-hand drive vehicles are not permitted
  • Emission standards: Euro 4 or higher required

Required Documents

  1. Commercial invoice and packing list
  2. Bill of lading
  3. Certificate of origin
  4. ESMA conformity certificate
  5. Insurance certificate
  6. Import license (for commercial importers)

Popular Chinese Brands in UAE Market

BYD

Leading EV manufacturer with affordable pricing and excellent battery technology. Models like the BYD Seal, Han, and Song Plus are increasingly popular in UAE.

NIO

Premium EV brand with battery swap technology. The ET7 and ES8 are gaining traction among luxury EV buyers in UAE.

Geely

Affordable and reliable vehicles under the Geely and Zeekr brands. The Geometry series offers great value for money.

Chery

One of the oldest Chinese automakers with a strong presence in Middle Eastern markets. Good balance of price and quality.

Shipping from China to UAE

The main shipping routes from China to UAE include:

  • Shanghai/Ningbo to Jebel Ali: 18-25 days transit time
  • Qingdao to Dubai: 20-28 days transit time
  • Tianjin to Sharjah: 18-24 days transit time

Shipping costs typically range from $800-$1,500 per vehicle depending on the port and container type (RO-RO or container).

Customs and Duties

UAE import duties for vehicles are generally 5% of the CIF value. However, there may be additional fees:

  • Customs clearance fees: AED 500-1,000
  • Port handling charges: AED 300-600
  • ESMA testing and certification: AED 1,000-3,000 per model
  • Registration and plate fees: AED 500-1,500

Tips for Successful Import

  1. Work with a reputable exporter: Choose suppliers with proven track records and proper documentation
  2. Verify vehicle history: Request service records and inspection reports
  3. Pre-shipment inspection: Consider third-party inspection services
  4. Understand warranty terms: Clarify after-sales support and parts availability
  5. Plan for registration: Ensure the vehicle meets local registration requirements
