Home / Buying guides / Export documents and vehicle shipping

The required document set depends on the vehicle, trade term, shipping route and destination country. A typical transaction may involve a commercial invoice, contract, vehicle registration or title records, export declaration and transport documents. Confirm the final list before payment.

How shipping works, step by step

  1. Choose your vehicle — browse our live inventory or ask for stock not yet listed.
  2. Get a written quote — vehicle price + shipping to your port + documentation, separated clearly.
  3. Pay securely — T/T bank transfer to the company account; the vehicle ships after payment confirmation.
  4. We export — export declaration, customs clearance and vessel booking are handled by our team.
  5. Receive at your port — collect the vehicle with the Bill of Lading and document set.

Typical transit times

  • Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Iraq): 20–35 days
  • Southeast Asia: 10–20 days
  • Africa (Nigeria, Kenya): 30–45 days
  • Central Asia (Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan): rail or drive-away, 15–30 days
  • South America / Europe: 35–50 days

Shipping method — container, RoRo or rail — depends on route availability, vehicle type, cost and destination handling.

Important notes for buyers

  • Import duties, taxes and destination-country registration are the buyer's responsibility — we provide the full export document set needed for customs clearance.
  • Vehicles are left-hand drive (LHD); please confirm your country accepts LHD imports.
  • Freight rates change with the market — ask us for an exact quote to your port this week.
