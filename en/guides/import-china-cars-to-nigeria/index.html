Nigerian dealers ask one question first: what will this car really cost at Apapa or Tin Can? This guide walks through landed-cost math for second hand cars from China — FOB Shenzhen price, ocean freight, the 20% duty + levies structure, and port charges — then shows where wholesale used cars from China with pre-inspected stock cut your biggest hidden cost: reconditioning surprises.

Landed cost formula. Landed = FOB vehicle price + ocean freight to Lagos + duty (20% of CIF value) + levies and surcharges + port, terminal and clearing fees. Work each unit against this formula before comparing suppliers: a car that looks $500 cheaper FOB can lose that edge if it arrives needing tyres, battery and bodywork.

Why used BYD cars for export top Nigerian demand lists. Fuel costs pushed buyers toward EVs and hybrids; our BYD Seagull starts at $6,639 FOB and DM-i hybrids bring petrol-range flexibility for intercity routes. Petrol SUVs (Haval H6, Chery Tiggo 8) still anchor family retail — a mixed container serves both segments.

Vehicle age rules. Nigeria applies additional tariff penalties to older vehicles; 2021–2024 stock sits in the lowest age band. Rules and rates change — always confirm current treatment with your clearing agent before committing to a purchase.

Bulk shipment vs single-unit: the margin difference. A 40ft container holds 4–6 SUVs or 8–10 compact cars. At 6+ units, shared freight typically cuts per-unit shipping cost by 30–45% versus loose consolidation. Combine that with pre-inspected stock and your reconditioning budget drops again — this double saving is where Nigerian lots stay profitable.

Document checklist for Nigerian clearing agents. Bill of Lading, commercial invoice, packing list, export declaration — issued to match your agent's filing template. Vehicles are LHD (left-hand drive); confirm current NCS acceptance for your model years.

How to verify pre-inspected stock remotely before payment. Every unit lists a photo-complete 168-point report: structure, verified odometer, tyre and brake condition, battery health for EVs, interior detail. Third-party inspection in Shenzhen before container stuffing is welcome — budget $150–250 per car and treat it as margin insurance.
